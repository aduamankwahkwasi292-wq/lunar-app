MrSOLIS 3 — Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Applies to: MrSOLIS 3 for Windows (from this website or the Microsoft Store) and for Android

In short: what you study stays on your computer or phone. MrSOLIS has no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The only things that leave it are what's needed to activate and check your licence, to sell you one, and to download the parts you choose.

1. What stays on your computer or phone

Everything you study with: the files you open, your lessons, questions and answers, notes, quiz results, history, and the photos and videos you attach. Voice is turned into text on your computer; it is never recorded or sent anywhere. The AI "brains" run on your computer or phone too.

It is kept in MrSOLIS's data folder (%APPDATA%\lunar-desktop). In the Microsoft Store version it is kept in the app's own storage, which Windows removes when you uninstall MrSOLIS. On Android it is kept in the app's own storage too, which Android removes when you uninstall MrSOLIS.

2. What we receive, and why

  • Activation and the daily licence check: your licence code, an anonymous device ID (a one-way code made from your computer's Windows ID or your phone's Android ID, which can't be turned back into it), the MrSOLIS version and the dates. We use these only to make sure each licence works on the computers and phones it allows.
  • Buying a licence: your email address (your receipt and licence code go there), the Mobile Money number you pay from (so we can match your payment), the anonymous device ID, and the order's reference, amount and dates. We use these to confirm your payment, give you your licence and handle refunds.
  • If you email us: your email address and message, to answer you.

We don't sell this information or use it for advertising. We share it only with the services below, or when the law requires us to.

3. Services that see some of it

  • Cloudflare runs our licence server, where the information in section 2 is stored. Like any web server, it sees your internet (IP) address when MrSOLIS connects; we use it only to block abuse in the moment and don't store it.
  • Your Mobile Money network handles your payment under its own terms.
  • GitHub and Hugging Face host the brains you choose to download, and GitHub hosts the downloads and updates from this website, including MrSOLIS for Android. Like any download, they see your IP address.
  • Microsoft installs and updates the Microsoft Store version, under its own privacy statement.

4. How long we keep it

Licence and order records are kept while your licence exists, and as long as we need them for payments, refunds and the law. You can ask us to delete your order details at any time; deleting a licence's record ends that licence.

5. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing jonsonwhocancreate@gmail.com. You also have rights under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

6. Students under 18

A parent or guardian must agree to the Terms of use for anyone under 18. We don't collect anything from anyone beyond what's listed above.

7. This website

This website has no analytics, cookies or adverts. To show the newest download, your browser asks GitHub for the latest MrSOLIS release.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: Behemoth Group, Ghana — jonsonwhocancreate@gmail.com.
